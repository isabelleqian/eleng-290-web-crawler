# Routing App Regulation Three Layer Summary

As of November 17, 2026. Federal review remains partial.

## Layer 1

Within the AB 2015 research scope. Routing app providers are expressly granted rights or assigned duties or liability.

Examples include proposed clearance-data protections for GPS administrators and enacted orders addressed to routing system operators.

11 indexed records with provisional classifications.

## Layer 2

Within the AB 2015 research scope. The principal legal subjects are other actors, and the provisions indirectly affect routing apps.

AB 2015 belongs here because Caltrans conducts the study and report. Road access, truck routes, traffic controls and network planning also principally govern other actors.

169 indexed records with provisional classifications.

## Layer 3

Other laws that may apply to routing apps outside the first two groups, assessed by their actual subject and operation.

General privacy, consumer protection and intermediary liability require separate analysis of applicability to the service or conduct.

9 indexed records with provisional classifications.

AB 2015 is Layer 2 and F3. The duty falls on Caltrans. General privacy and intermediary liability records are assessed in Layer 3. The full index contains 189 instrument records and 10 court decisions. Federal records with missing amendment, penalty or effective-date fields remain explicitly pending.
